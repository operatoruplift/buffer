How this works

Incremental P&L = signed size × fixed price × (price move ÷ 100)

A −10% move produces −1,500 USDC for the SOL long and +5,000 USDC for the BTC short, totaling +3,500 USDC. A +10% move reverses those signs.

Both prices move by the same percentage. Size stays fixed. This excludes funding, fees, slippage, orders, collateral price changes, debt, margin changes and liquidation. It is not a forecast, account valuation, or liquidation estimate.
